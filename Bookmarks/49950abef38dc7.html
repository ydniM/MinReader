Nvidia stock rose more than 30% after the company’s board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive and president.

He resigned nearly two weeks ago following reports of extramarital affairs.

Donald Trump said he spoke to the Nvidia board directly about reinstating Huang. The president’s portfolio has millions invested in Nvidia stock, but the White House has denied that Trump has any influence over his investment accounts.

US lawmakers on both sides of the aisle are questioning the board’s decision. Huang is scheduled to testify at a House hearing on December 1st and before the Senate on December 3rd.

"The board believes Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," Nvidia chair Harvey Jones said.

"We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the Company. We are grateful to Colette Kress for her outstanding service as interim CEO, in addition to her continuing responsibilities as Nvidia's CFO."

"I am honored to return to Nvidia and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution," Huang said in a statement.
