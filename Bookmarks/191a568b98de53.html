-Christina Ricci who is promoting Yellowjackets, revealed on "Watch What Happens Live" that she does not keep in touch with her former costar in the 1990 movie "Mermaids."

"Unfortunately, she's still mad at me," Ricci said of Cher. "She holds a grudge." Cher was interviewed about Ricci for a magazine when shew as 19, and Cher said "all these things that were not that amazing" about her. Cher felt she was misquoted, so she left Ricci a voicemail "about how journalists are always trying to get the worst things" and saying she was "so sorry."

-Ricci stated that she never returned the call, and she believes Cher is still angry at her as a result. "I'm pretty sure," Ricci said. "I've reached out to her since and gotten radio silence."

-She went on to say she has no ill will toward Cher, telling host Andy Cohen that "I adore her" and "she's the best." She also said she can understand Cher's perspective on the situation.
