Watch as Vogue's "Best Dressed" cover star Connor Storrie puts together a protein-packed deconstructed hamburger with a mezcal Mandy. The burger, made with ground beef, tomatoes, and arugula, is one of Connor’s real-life everyday meals. As he prepares his food, Connor talks about the evolution of his Canadian “eh” since starting work on Heated Rivalry. Along the way, Connor karate-chops his beef, takes a bite out of a set-dressed eggplant, recalls sitting next to Madonna and Kate Moss at a fashion show, and comments on the internet’s reaction to his new haircut.
