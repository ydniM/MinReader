NEW: A federal judge has blocked the Trump regime's FCC from proceeding with its early license renewal proceedings against all eight ABC-owned television stations, finding that ABC and Disney are likely to succeed in proving the regime retaliated against them for exercising their First Amendment rights.

Judge Loren AliKhan rejected the FCC’s justification for its actions as not credible, detailing a pattern of threats by Trump and FCC Chairman Brendan Carr targeting ABC’s news coverage, Jimmy Kimmel and The View.

“This is not a close case,” the judge wrote.

-Follow up to this post

-The Judge overseeing the ABC v FCC first amendment lawsuit has partially sided with ABC and has put a halt to the Agency’s Early License Renewal Proceeding.

-In her ruling Judge Loren AliKhan stated “No one doubts that the Commission may enforce the law in the manner it sees fit, but in doing so, Defendants may not retaliate against or punish Plaintiffs and other broadcast license holders for disfavored speech.”

-She also rejected the FCC’s effort to dismiss the lawsuit on jurisdictional grounds stating “Given the extreme, indeed existential, nature of the possible penalty Plaintiffs face – loss of their broadcast licenses – the court concludes that early license renewal proceedings create a risk sufficient to chill a broadcaster of ordinary firmness,”

-Trump’s attacks and warnings against the network did the FCC no favors as the judge noted that three of the co-hosts of The View, as well as Kimmel and ABC News reporters have entries on the White House’s “media offenders” webpage. She said that Trump and Carr’s “call and response” was “no coincidence.” “What is more, Chairman Carr has taken President Trump’s rhetoric as his marching orders. The same week he was tapped to be President Trump’s FCC chair, following months of President Trump’s calling for ‘ABC [to] lose its license’ for how it covered the presidential campaign …. then-Commissioner Carr lauded ‘license revocation’ as a ‘remedy’ to ‘deter this kind of conduct.'”

-The Judge also denied ABC’s request for a broader injunction that would prohibit any FCC actions “to coerce or threaten” ABC with sanctions as it has not happened yet.

-The FCC is of course pushing back against the judgement
