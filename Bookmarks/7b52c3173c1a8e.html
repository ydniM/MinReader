Grand Theft Auto VI is a true monocultural event. Every news channel is covering it, anyone with a passing interest in video games is playing it, and it’s made almost $4 billion in less than a week. Desperate to connect with constituents, governments around the world have tried to capitalize on GTA’s popularity by releasing some of the worst AI-generated memes I’ve ever seen. The pictures of politicians in the hyper-real cartoon pink-tinted style of GTA 6 are so awful they even caused a minor political scandal in Scotland.

Let’s begin in the United States, where Vice President JD Vance, the White House, and several other government-run X accounts attempted to capture the cultural zeitgeist by quickly churning out AI-generated meme garbage. GTA has long been popular with Trump 2.0 officials. In March, the official X account of the White House published a compilation of strikes on Iranian targets overlaid with the “WASTED” game over screen from GTA. 

In one of the more embarrassing exchanges, the X account for House Republicans posted an AI-generated picture of Hakeem Jefferies, Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, and Abdul El-Sayed above the words “Grand Theft Taxpayer.” A Democrat-linked account then replied with its own picture of Trump and the words “Grand Theft Autocrat.” Embarrassing for all involved.

Israel also got in on the action. On the Friday after the game’s release, U.S. ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee shared a “Shabbot Shalom” meme in the GTA style. The Israel Defense Force’s Farsi X account posted several pictures of dead Iranian officials captioned as “WASTED.”

“We saw all this killing before GTA6,” the post said in Persian, referring to the now retired meme we got something before GTA 6. This account posts in Farsi and meant for “direct dialogue between us and you,” meaning Israel and Iran, according to its bio on X.

In India, Prime Minister Narenda Modi and his Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) posted GTA memes repeatedly on November 19, the day the game was released.

Indian politician and frequent Modi critic Jairam Ramesh mocked Modi for the posts on social media. “The PM in a desperate bid to reach out to the youth has resorted to GTA V and GTA VI imagery on social media,” Ramesh said in his post. “Recall that this video game follows a criminal duo. How very appropriate.”

On Instagram, Singapore’s Land Transport Authority made fun of people who can’t navigate the country’s rail system. “If he can memorize the entire GTA6 map, he can memorize the Singapore MRT System Map,” the post said. “Bro knows every corner of Leonida but still saves the SG MRT System Map on his phone to get to Orchard. 💀”

NATO posted its attempt at being part of the cultural conversation on Instagram. “Wanted level: NATO,” the post said above the pink-hued cross cut collage of military images in the GTA style. “The final boss of wanted levels. Think you could fend off the most successful Alliance in history? NATO isn’t part of GTA6 of course, but if it was, we think even the best gamers would have a challenge with this one.”

In Scotland, the Scottish National Party (SNP) posted GTA 6 memes so cringe that they talked about it on the floor of parliament. On November 19, the SNP social media accounts posted AI-generated pictures of Boris Johnson and Margaret Thatcher in the GTA style. “When the loading screen so long you start seeing characters you’ve never seen before,” one image said over a picture of Thatcher pouring a bag of “Scottish oil money” into the UK treasury.

Scotland’s Green Party didn’t find the memes funny. Rockstar is originally Scottish, was founded in Edinburgh, and still maintains a significant studio there. In parliament, Green co-leader Gillian Mackay said she was proud of what all of Rockstar’s workers had done to stand up against bad working conditions and produce a work of art like GTA 6. Then she made comments about SNP leader and Scottish First Minister John Swiney. 

“Last night the SNP angered anti-AI campaigners by posting AI-generated GTA memes on its social media despite huge concerns about the impact of AI and AI data centers on artists, workers, and the environment,” Mackay said in the parliament session. “Does the first minister understand why people are concerned about his government’s approach to AI and the big companies behind it.”

The SNP deleted the memes and Swiney apologized. “First of all, let me say that I don’t want to offend anyone by anything that we get up to, so if I’ve offended anyone about that, I’m sorry about that. It was not my intention,” Swiney said in the Scottish parliament. Then he said he shared concerns about AI but defended the technology’s use for healthcare.

There will be more bad posts like this. Grand Theft Auto VI hasn’t been out for even a week and the memetic landscape around it will shift and change. We don’t even know what the dominant memes for the game are yet, let alone the innovative ways posters will iterate on them. Rest assured, however, that the governments of the world are watching and waiting to post their own variations and make it all cringe.
