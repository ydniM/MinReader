Here's a 2 for 1 special on Love is Dad.

First up, JoJo Siwa and Chris Hughes have broken up after a year and a half. “JoJo is really hurt. She loved him and gave everything she had to make the relationship work,” a friend of Siwa told PEOPLE. “For months, behind closed doors, she kept hoping things would change. But eventually, she realized that she deserved to feel just as loved and supported as she wanted him to feel.”

The source adds: “Walking away wasn’t easy, and it doesn’t mean she stopped caring about him. She just knew she needed to choose herself. She genuinely wishes him well.”

Up next, Thom Evans announced on socials that after 7 years together, his engagement with Nicole Scherzinger has come to an end. He wrote, "It is with a heavy heart that I share that Nicole and I are no longer together. After seven years of love, commitment and believing in a future together, this is an incredibly difficult time. Whilst this is not the outcome I had ever hoped for, I respect Nicole's decision and genuinely wish her every happiness in the next chapter of her life."
