Macy’s 100th Thanksgiving Day parade will be pushed back to Saturday because of severe weather, officials said.

“The safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority, and after close consultation with our City and agency partners, we have made the decision to move this year’s celebration,” Macy’s said Wednesday.

The postponement came as the East Coast was bracing to be hit by severe weather on Thanksgiving Day, bringing heavy rain, and strong winds, according to the National Weather Service.

The parade will now start at 8:30 a.m. ET Saturday.
