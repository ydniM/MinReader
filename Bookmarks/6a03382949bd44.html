‘Steven Universe’ Star Zyri Comes Out as Trans https://t.co/IzjWa5nxbZ

— Variety (@Variety) November 25, 2026


Zyri, who voiced the titular character in “Steven Universe” and played Brian Corbett on “The Goldbergs,” has come out as trans.

“Coming to terms with my dysphoria and my transness was only a small piece of my journey towards being a whole, authentic person, but it was notable as the first thing that truly felt like mine in my entire life,” Zyri wrote. “That’s also why for a time I was so terrified of being outed: I didn’t want to lose my newfound light the way I’d lost control of my name, my childhood, and my entire identity to the brand I was molded into by others. I know now that I really had nothing to fear there — the only thing that defines me is me — but I’ve had to continuously unlearn being afraid of losing all of myself again. That’s probably something I’ll have to remind myself of for the rest of my life, but at least now, for the very first time, I can envision the rest of my life.”
